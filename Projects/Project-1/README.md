# OVERVIEW
This Python script is a command-line based Water Intake Tracker designed to help you monitor and meet your daily hydration goals. At its core, the program uses a personalized formula to calculate how much water you should be drinking each day: it takes your body weight in pounds, multiplies it by 0.5 to get a base ounce goal, and then adds 8 ounces for every hour of exercise you've done that day. So for example, if you weigh 180 lbs and exercised for 1.5 hours, your daily goal would be 102 ounces. Once your goal is set, you can log water intake either by entering a custom amount in ounces or by using the "Quick Add" option which instantly logs 8 ounces — useful for tracking a standard glass of water. As you log water, the program keeps a running total and will alert you with a congratulations message the moment you hit or exceed your goal.

# HOW TO USE
Beyond just logging water, the script also handles day-to-day state management intelligently. It stores the current date when the program runs and automatically detects when a new calendar day has begun, resetting your consumption back to zero so you always start fresh — though you can also trigger a manual reset at any time from the menu. When the program launches, you are presented with a numbered menu of six options. Selecting 1 allows you to set your daily goal by entering your weight and hours of exercise, which the program uses to calculate your personalized target. Selecting 2 lets you log a custom amount of water in ounces, while selecting 3 is a quick add that instantly logs 8 ounces without any extra input. Selecting 4 brings up your progress screen, showing your goal, total consumed, remaining ounces, and your percentage toward completion. Selecting 5 manually resets your daily consumption back to zero if needed, and selecting 6 exits the program entirely.
