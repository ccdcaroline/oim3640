## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:** A Python program that analyzes how repetitive Taylor Swift songs are by measuring word frequency and repetition patterns across a few of her lyrics.
**Why I chose this:** I'm one of the very few people that don't like Taylor Swift's music, so I wanted to see if her songs were repetitive. 
**Core features:** 
- Load and clean song lyrics (remove punctuation and common words like “the”, “and”)
- Count word frequency using Python dictionaries
- Identify the most common words in each song
- Calculate repetition (percentage of repeated vs unique words)
**What I don't know yet:** 
- How to properly clean text (removing punctuation, lowercase, etc)
- The best way to ignore common “filler” words (stopwords)
- How to calculate repetition percentage clearly
- Whether I should analyze full songs or just verses/choruses
