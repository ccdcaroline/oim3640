# My Project Proposal: Mini Project 1 


* **What I'm building:** Water intake tracker. 

* **Why I chose this:** I struggle to drink enough water and I want to create a clear path to reach my daily water intake goal. 

* **Core features:** Plug in how much water you drink during the day to let you know how much water is left and when you have completed the amount of water needed. 

* **What I don't know yet:** How much water a person needs to drink per day? How much it varies based off gender and weight? What would factor into how much water someone would need? Should I keep it to one metric system or vary?
